🌷 About Sifen

Every person has a story. Here is a little piece of hers.

Sifen Dula is a passionate IT student, a creative thinker, and someone who is always looking for new things to learn. She was named Best Delegate in a debate held on 3/11/25 and also serves as a judge for the MUN club. But there is much more to her than achievements. She has dreams, interests, experiences, and a personality that makes her who she is.

The meaning behind her name ✨

Sifen means "for you," and Dula means "to battle."

Together, her name carries the beautiful meaning: "I'll battle for you." ⚔️
